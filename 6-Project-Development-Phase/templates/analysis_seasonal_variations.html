Seasonal Analysis Overview

This analysis examines seasonal electricity consumption patterns across different regions and time periods. By understanding seasonal variations, we can better predict demand, optimize grid operations, and plan for peak usage periods. The analysis includes state-wise comparisons, regional heatmaps, and trend forecasting.

Key Insights from Seasonal Analysis

Summer Peak Patterns

Consistent summer peaks observed across all states, with southern states showing higher consumption due to longer cooling seasons.

Monsoon Variations

Monsoon season shows moderate consumption with regional variations based on agricultural and industrial activities.

Winter Patterns

Northern states show higher winter consumption due to heating requirements, while southern states maintain moderate usage.

Forecasting Indicators

Historical patterns provide reliable indicators for future seasonal demand planning and grid optimization.
