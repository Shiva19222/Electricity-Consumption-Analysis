Story Insights

Consumption Patterns

The story reveals clear seasonal patterns with summer peaks, regional variations showing North-South divides, and significant COVID-19 impact during lockdown periods.

Geographic Insights

Metropolitan areas show higher consumption patterns, while regional analysis highlights infrastructure and economic development correlations with electricity usage.

Pandemic Impact

COVID-19 lockdowns caused significant consumption drops, particularly in commercial and industrial sectors, while residential consumption saw increases during work-from-home periods.

Sector Analysis

Different sectors show varying consumption patterns with industrial areas having the highest demand, followed by commercial and residential sectors with distinct daily and seasonal patterns.
