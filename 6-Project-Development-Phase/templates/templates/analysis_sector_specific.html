Sector-Specific Analysis Overview

This analysis focuses on understanding electricity consumption patterns across different sectors, regions, and geographical areas. By examining usage by region, top consuming states, metropolitan areas, and geographic distributions, we can identify sector-specific patterns and develop targeted conservation programs.

Key Insights from Sector-Specific Analysis

Industrial vs Residential

Industrial states show higher per-capita consumption compared to residential states, indicating sector-specific usage patterns.

Urban Concentration

Metropolitan areas demonstrate significantly higher consumption due to commercial and industrial activities.

Regional Disparities

Clear regional disparities observed with western and southern states showing higher consumption than eastern states.

Conservation Opportunities

High-consuming sectors identified for targeted conservation programs and efficiency initiatives.
