Key Insights

Seasonal Consumption Patterns

Analysis reveals clear seasonal patterns with peak consumption during summer months (April-July) due to increased air conditioning usage, and lower consumption during winter months. The monsoon season shows moderate consumption patterns.

Regional Variations

Significant regional variations exist, with northern and western states showing higher consumption patterns compared to eastern and northeastern regions. This correlates with industrial development and population density patterns.

COVID-19 Impact Analysis

The COVID-19 lockdown period (March-May 2020) showed a significant drop in electricity consumption, particularly in commercial and industrial sectors. Residential consumption saw a slight increase due to work-from-home arrangements.

Metro vs Non-Metro Consumption

Metropolitan cities consistently show higher per-capita electricity consumption compared to non-metro areas. This is attributed to higher living standards, greater appliance usage, and commercial activity concentration.

Monthly Trends

Monthly analysis shows consistent patterns with peak consumption in May-June and lower consumption in November-December. Weekend consumption patterns differ from weekday patterns, with reduced industrial activity on weekends.
