Time Pattern Analysis Overview

This analysis focuses on understanding electricity consumption patterns across different time dimensions. By examining monthly trends, quarterly variations, and yearly comparisons, we can identify peak usage periods, seasonal patterns, and long-term trends that help in grid management and planning.

Key Insights from Time Pattern Analysis

Peak Usage Periods

Analysis reveals clear peak consumption during summer months (April-July) due to increased air conditioning usage.

Seasonal Variations

Significant seasonal variations observed with lower consumption during winter months and moderate usage during monsoon season.

COVID-19 Impact

2020 shows distinct pattern changes during lockdown periods, particularly in Q2, affecting overall consumption trends.

Growth Trends

Year-over-year analysis reveals overall growth trends with some states showing significant increases in consumption.
